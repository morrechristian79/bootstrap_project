About Me

Hello! I'm Christian Morre, an aspiring Full Stack Web Developer with a passion for building modern, user-friendly web applications. I am currently developing a variety of projects that will serve as my professional portfolio, allowing me to continuously expand my skills in both front-end and back-end development. Prior to pursuing web development, I worked as a Senior Systems Administrator and currently serve as a Service Delivery Coordinator for a US-based IT firm. My background in IT operations, systems management, and service delivery has provided me with a strong technical foundation and a deep understanding of business and technology requirements. As I explore a career transition, I have chosen Full Stack Web Development as my preferred path, driven by my passion for creating innovative digital solutions. To support this journey, I am actively working on various web development projects, continuously learning new technologies, and gaining hands-on experience in designing and building websites and web applications. I am excited to grow as a developer and contribute to meaningful projects that create value for businesses and users alike.
